Of Mice and Men Unseen Poetry Exam Tips

Of Mice and Men

Overview: A novel by John Steinbeck about the American Dream, friendship, loneliness, and power dynamics during the Great Depression.

Themes:

  • The American Dream
  • Loneliness and Isolation
  • Friendship
  • Power and Disempowerment

Characters:

  • George: Smart and protective
  • Lennie: Childlike, strong, mentally disabled
  • Crooks: Isolated, black stable-hand
  • Curley's Wife: Lonely, misunderstood

Key Quotes:

“Guys like us, that work on ranches, are the loneliest guys in the world.”
“I got you to look after me, and you got me to look after you.”
“A guy needs somebody - to be near him.”

Sample Essay Tip:

Always link your quotes to the context (e.g., 1930s America, Great Depression) and themes. Use PEE paragraphs (Point, Evidence, Explain).

Unseen Poetry

What to do:

  • Read the poem twice
  • Highlight keywords, techniques (similes, metaphors, personification)
  • Write about how the poet presents emotions, ideas, or relationships
  • Structure answer in paragraphs, using evidence from the poem

Common Techniques:

  • Imagery
  • Enjambment
  • Alliteration
  • Symbolism

Mini Practice:

“The wind whispered through the trees, like voices from another world.”

What technique is used? How does it create atmosphere?

Exam Tips

  • Always plan before you write
  • Underline key words in the question
  • Include context for *Of Mice and Men*
  • Quote, explain, and zoom in on language
  • Practice timing – 45 minutes for each section
