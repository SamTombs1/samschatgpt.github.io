Unseen Poetry Techniques

Match the technique to its effect:

  • Simile: Compares two things using 'like' or 'as'
  • Personification: Gives human qualities to non-human things
  • Enjambment: Lines run over without punctuation → flow or emotion
  • Alliteration: Repeating consonants → creates rhythm or mood
  • Imagery: Appeals to senses → paints a picture

Did You Know?

Fun Fact: In *Of Mice and Men*, Steinbeck based the character of Lennie on a friend he had growing up. The friend had a mental disability and was much stronger than others.

Tip: When studying for *Of Mice and Men*, focus on the significance of the American Dream. It’s a core theme that will likely come up in your exam questions!
